Privacy · updated 2026-10-16

What we keep, and what we don't

Plain language, no lawyer-speak. The library is built to hold as little about you as it can.

No account, no sign-in

You can browse, upload, and download without making an account. We don't ask for your name, email, or a password, and there is nothing to log in to.

What an upload stores

When you upload a file, the server keeps three things: the file itself, any files found inside it when it is unpacked, and the written findings from inspecting it. Uploads are public — anyone can browse and download them. Uploaded programs are never run.

How long it stays

On the upload form you choose what happens next:

  • Delete after 24 hours (the default): about a day after you upload, the whole thing — the file, anything unpacked from it, and its findings — is removed automatically and cannot be recovered.
  • Keep: the upload stays until an operator removes it. Once an upload is published you cannot delete it yourself; ask through Report a file.

An upload you start but never finish is deleted after about an hour without new data.

Either way, there is a limit of 1 GiB per upload, and you confirm the file is yours to share and holds no personal data or secrets before it goes up.

Stopping abuse

To keep one visitor from filling the library or slowing it for everyone, the server counts recent requests, uploads, and downloaded bytes for each network address. Those counts live only in the server's memory, reset within an hour, and are never written to disk or shared. Separately, our hosting provider, Railway, keeps standard request logs, such as the IP address and the page requested, to run the service.

Analytics

We use PostHog to count page views and a few clicks (like opening a file or starting an upload) so we can see what people find useful. It is set up to respect you:

  • It runs in cookieless mode — no cookies, and nothing stored on your device between visits.
  • No session recording, no screen capture, no reading of what you type.
  • No personal profiles. We never put file names, hashes, or file contents into an analytics event.
  • Data is sent to PostHog's United States region.
  • If your browser sends "Do Not Track," analytics stays off. It is also off entirely unless the operator has turned it on.

No ad or social trackers

There are no advertising pixels, no social-media trackers, and no third-party scripts loaded from other sites. A strict content-security policy blocks them.
