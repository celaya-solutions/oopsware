Terms of use

Short and plain. By using the library you agree to these.

Only upload what you may share

The library does not host illegal material or anything that infringes someone's copyright. By uploading, you confirm that you have the right to share the file and that it contains no personal data or secrets. You agree to this on the upload form before anything goes up.

Everything you upload is public

Anyone can browse and download your uploads and the files found inside them. Don't upload anything you are not willing to make public.

Files are inspected, never run

The service inspects files statically — it reads them. It never executes an uploaded program. Inspection does not and cannot promise a file is safe or free of malware. Treat downloaded files with the same care as anything else from the internet.

Removal

The operator may remove any upload at any time, for any reason. If you believe something here infringes your rights or contains personal data, ask us to take it down — see Report a file. We remove infringing or personal-data files promptly.

As-is, no warranty

The library is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It may be unavailable, lose data, or change at any time. Findings are best-effort and may be incomplete or wrong; the service separates what it observed from what it could not determine, and you should verify anything that matters.

The software

Oopsware is an independent fork of an open-source project. New Oopsware work is for noncommercial use; upstream code keeps its original license. See About for credits and the license.
