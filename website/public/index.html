Oopsware

Well, now we know.

Bring an app, a binary, a package, or a wonderfully odd bit of behavior. We’re here to look inside, follow the clues, and learn something.

The implementation details appear to have fallen out.

01

Put it on the bench.

A local workbench, a terminal command, and tools for your agent. Inspect files, trace a function, compare two versions, or ask why a button does that. 

node scripts/oopsware.mjs web --port 4097

Build from source first. The README walks through it. No published package is needed.

02

Keep the clues.

“I saw this,” “I think this,” and “I don’t know yet” are different things. Oopsware keeps evidence and limits with the result, so the next question has somewhere solid to start.

Bring your own analysis tools when a target needs them. Bring your own questions, always.
